# Customer Sentiment Analysis of Beauty & Wellness Product Reviews

## Week 1 – Data Science Internship

### Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer reviews of beauty and wellness products using Data Science and Natural Language Processing (NLP).

### Business Problem
Beauty and wellness businesses receive a large number of customer reviews. It can be difficult to manually analyze all reviews and identify customer opinions and recurring problems.

### Objective
The main objective is to analyze customer reviews and classify them into:
- Positive
- Neutral
- Negative

### Research Questions
1. What proportion of reviews are positive, neutral, and negative?
2. Which product categories receive more negative feedback?
3. What common topics appear in customer reviews?
4. Is there a relationship between star ratings and sentiment?

### Proposed Tools
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Scikit-learn
